Privacy Policy

WordNet Grid - Word Puzzle Game

Kid-Friendly Age 6+

Last Updated: December 5, 2024

Welcome to WordNet Grid! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you play our word puzzle game. We care about your privacy and safety! 🛡️

🎯 Who We Are

WordNet Grid is developed by Zenith Code Studio. We create fun and educational games for everyone!

✅ Our Kid-Safe Promise

WordNet Grid is designed to be safe for children ages 6 and older.

  • 🚫 We do NOT collect personal information from children
  • 🚫 We do NOT require account creation or login
  • 🚫 We do NOT have chat or communication features
  • 🚫 We do NOT share any data with third parties for tracking

📱 Information We Access

Our game may access the following to make your experience better:

  • Game Progress: Your scores and completed levels are saved on your device only
  • Google Play Games (Optional): If you choose to sign in, your scores may be shared on leaderboards
  • Advertisements: We show ads from Google AdMob. These ads are set to be child-appropriate

📢 About Advertisements

We use Google AdMob to show ads in our game. For users under 18, we request child-directed ads (COPPA compliant). These ads:

  • Are reviewed for child-appropriateness
  • Do not use behavioral targeting on children
  • May use contextual information (not personal data)

🎮 Google Play Games Services

If you choose to use Google Play Games features (leaderboards, achievements), Google's privacy policy applies. This is optional and not required to play the game.

💾 Data Storage

All game data (scores, level progress, settings) is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data.

🔒 Data Security

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Your game progress stays on your device!

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Parents' Guide

Dear Parents, WordNet Grid is designed with your child's safety in mind:

  • No in-app purchases that children can make accidentally
  • No social features or user-generated content
  • No collection of personal data
  • Ads are filtered for child-appropriateness

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top. We encourage you to check back occasionally.
